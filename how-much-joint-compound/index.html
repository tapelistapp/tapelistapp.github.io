How much joint compound do I need?

Short answer: about 0.042 L per square foot of drywall, or 1 gallon per 90 sq ft, for taping and finishing. A 12 x 12 ft room, walls and ceiling: 21.45 L, two 17 L pails. A 10 x 12 ft room: 19.11 L, 1 pail + 2 small tubs.

How to work it out

1. Board area of the walls: the perimeter times the wall height, minus half of each door and window (the sheets go over them and get cut out). A 12 x 12 ft room with 8 ft walls, one door and one window: 384 − 15 = 369 sq ft.

2. Add the ceiling if you tape it too: 144 sq ft. Total 513 sq ft.

3. Multiply by about 0.042 L per sq ft (0.45 L per m²): 513 × 0.042 = 21.45 L.

4. Round up to real containers. One 17 L pail is 4.45 L short, and two 2 L tubs would not cover it, so buy 2 pails.

Questions

Pail or small tubs?

If you need a little more than one pail, one or two 2 L tubs cover the rest with less left over. A 10 x 12 room needs 19.11 L: 1 pail and 2 tubs leave 1.9 L, two pails would leave 15 L. More than two tubs over, and a second pail is the better buy.

What about the tape?
