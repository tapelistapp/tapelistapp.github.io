How to work out how much drywall you need

1. Multiply the perimeter of the room by the wall height. A 14 x 12 ft room with 8 ft walls has 416 sq ft of wall.

2. Take off half of the doors and windows. You hang full sheets over most openings and cut them out, so the cut-outs rarely come back as pieces you can use.

3. Add 10% for waste in a square room, 15% for an L-shape or a room with many corners, and divide by the area of one sheet: 32 sq ft for 4 x 8, 40 for 4 x 10, 54 for 54 in x 12 ft.

4. Pick the sheet by wall height so you do not hang a thin strip at the top: 4 x 8 ft for 8 ft walls, 54 in x 12 ft for 9 ft walls, 4 x 10 ft hung vertically for 10 ft walls.

Questions

How many screws per sheet?

About one per square foot of board, so 32 on a 4 x 8 sheet on the wall and more on the ceiling, where the spacing is tighter. Use 1-1/4 in coarse-thread screws for 1/2 in board on wood studs.

How much joint compound do I need?

About 0.45 litres per square metre of board (roughly a 17 L pail for 400 sq ft) for the tape coat, two finish coats and some loss.
