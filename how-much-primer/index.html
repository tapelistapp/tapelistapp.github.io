How much primer do I need?

Short answer: measure the walls (and the ceiling if it is new drywall too), take off doors and windows, allow about 350 sq ft per gallon for one coat and add 10%. New drywall in a 12 x 12 ft room, walls and ceiling: 2 gallons.

How to work it out

1. Wall area: the perimeter times the wall height. A 12 x 12 ft room with 8 ft walls has 48 ft of wall, so 384 sq ft.

2. Take off the doors and windows: about 18 sq ft for a 32 x 80 in door and 12 sq ft for a 36 x 48 in window. 384 − 18 − 12 = 354 sq ft.

3. New drywall on the ceiling? Add length x width: 144 sq ft. Total 498 sq ft.

4. One coat at about 350 sq ft per gallon (8.6 m² per litre), plus 10% for the roller and the tray: 5.9 litres. Round up to whole cans: 2 gallons.

Questions

When do I need primer?

On new drywall and on patched or skim-coated spots, which soak up paint unevenly. Over sound, already painted walls you can usually go straight to paint.

Is primer the same as paint?

No: it is a separate product with its own coverage, so it is a separate line on the list. Paint goes on top, usually two coats.
