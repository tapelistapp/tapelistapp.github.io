One list for the whole job

Paint, drywall and trim for every room of the project, added up and rounded once.

Paint

Walls, ceiling, primer, coats, an accent wall and the color of each wall, in quarts, gallons and 5-gallon pails.

Drywall

Sheets sized to the wall height, screws, joint compound, tape and corner bead.

Trim

Baseboard, door and window trim, quarter round: pieces in store lengths, caulk, nails and filler.

Measure like you do on site

Rectangle, L-shape, a single wall or any shape you draw. Doors, windows and closets go on the wall where they really are. Type sizes on a keypad made for tape-measure numbers, like 12' 6" or 4 3/16", or switch to metres.

Every number explained

Tap "Why this amount?" to see the areas, the coverage, the waste and how it was rounded to real packages. Change any amount yourself; the app keeps it and tells you when the room changes. Share the list as text or as an Excel file with live formulas.
