How much baseboard do I need?

Short answer: add up the wall lengths, take off the door openings, add 10% for cuts and corners, and round up to whole pieces. A 12 x 12 ft room with one door needs 7 pieces of 8 ft.

How to work it out

1. Add up the length of every wall. A 12 x 12 ft room has 4 × 12 = 48 ft.

2. Take off each door opening. A 32 in door is 2.7 ft, so 48 − 2.7 = 45.3 ft. Windows stay in: the baseboard runs under them.

3. Add 10% for miter cuts at the corners, joints and mistakes: 45.3 + 4.5 = 49.9 ft.

4. Divide by the length of one piece and round up. 6 pieces of 8 ft give 48 ft, which is short, so buy 7.

Questions

Should I buy longer pieces?

Longer pieces mean fewer joints on a long wall, but they are harder to carry and cut. TapeList lets you pick 8, 12 or 16 ft pieces and counts the pieces for that length.

What about door trim?

Door casing is a separate item: count it per door, not per foot of wall. TapeList adds it as its own line when you pick the door and window trim task.
