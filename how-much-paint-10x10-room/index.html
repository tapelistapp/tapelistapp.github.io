How much paint for a 10x10 room?

Short answer: a 10 x 10 ft bedroom with 8 ft walls, one door and one window needs 2 gallons of wall paint for two coats, and 1 gallon of ceiling paint.

Video: paint for a 10 x 10 bedroom

38 seconds: the room in the app, the wall area, two coats and the cans.

How to work it out

1. Wall area: the perimeter times the wall height. 4 × 10 ft × 8 ft = 320 sq ft.

2. Take off the door and the window: about 18 sq ft for a 32 x 80 in door and 12 sq ft for a 36 x 48 in window. 320 − 30 = 290 sq ft.

3. Two coats: 580 sq ft. Add 10% for cutting in, the roller and the tray.

4. About 375 sq ft per gallon (9.2 m² per litre): 6.45 L. Round up to real cans: 2 gallons, with about 0.9 L left for touch-ups.

5. Ceiling: 100 sq ft, two coats, plus 10%: 2.2 L. 1 gallon of ceiling paint.

Questions

Is one gallon enough for a 10 x 10 room?

For one coat on the walls, yes. Most rooms need two coats, especially when the color changes, and that is 2 gallons.

Gallon or quart for the rest?

If you need a little over 2 gallons, a quart usually covers it with less left over than a third gallon. The 9 ft walls version above is 2 gallons + 1 quart.
