# TapeList Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-10-25 (first published 2026-10-04)

This policy explains what data the Android app **TapeList: Materials Calculator** ("TapeList", "the app") handles.
The app is published on Google Play by the developer **TheGreenStudio** ("we", "us").

## Contact

For any privacy question or request, email **tapelistmaterialscalculator@gmail.com**.

## The app collects no data

TapeList collects no data and sends nothing over the internet. It has no crash reporting, no analytics, no ads and
no identifiers, and it does not even have permission to use the internet.

Google Play may show us counts of crashes and "app not responding" events from users who chose, in their Android
settings, to share usage and diagnostics data with Google. That data is gathered by Google under Google's own privacy
policy, not by the app.

## Your projects stay on your device

TapeList has no account and no sign-in. Your projects, rooms, measurements, names, notes, paint colors and shopping
lists are stored only on your device. The app does not send them to us or to anyone else.

When you share a shopping list or export it to Excel, the app hands it to the app or place you choose
(for example a messenger or your files). We do not receive a copy.

## Feedback email

If you send us feedback from the app, it opens your own email app with our address and the app version in the
subject; you write and send the email. We receive what you write and your email address. We use them only to answer
you, keep them only as long as needed for that, and delete them sooner on request.

## Deleting your data

- **Feedback emails**: the only data of yours we hold. To have them deleted, write to the contact address above.
- **Data on your device**: uninstalling the app, or clearing its data in Android settings, removes your projects
  and settings from the device.

## Android backup

Android may back up the app's data (your projects, settings and the early-user status) to your Google account,
according to your own Android backup settings. This backup is handled by Google under your account settings; we
cannot see it.

## Children

TapeList is not directed at children.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date.

- 2026-10-25: the app no longer uses Firebase Crashlytics and sends no crash reports; it collects no data.
- 2026-10-04: first version.
