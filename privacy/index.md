# TapeList Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-10-04

This policy explains what data the Android app **TapeList: Materials Calculator** ("TapeList", "the app") handles.
The app is published on Google Play by the developer **TheGreenStudio** ("we", "us").

## Contact

For any privacy question or request, email **tapelistmaterialscalculator@gmail.com**.

## Your projects stay on your device

TapeList has no account and no sign-in. Your projects, rooms, measurements, names, notes, paint colors and shopping
lists are stored only on your device. The app does not send them to us or to anyone else.

When you share a shopping list or export it to Excel, the app hands it to the app or place you choose
(for example a messenger or your files). We do not receive a copy.

TapeList shows no ads and contains no advertising or analytics SDK.

## Crash reports (Firebase Crashlytics)

To find and fix errors, TapeList uses Firebase Crashlytics, a service of Google. When the app crashes or hits an
error, it may send a crash report that contains:

- **Crash logs**: the stack trace of the error.
- **Diagnostics**: the state of the app at the time of the error, and device metadata (such as the device model,
  the Android version and the app version).
- **Other app performance data**: app usage metrics collected by the Firebase Sessions SDK (such as the time the
  app comes to the foreground and a session starts), and the user agent.
- **Device or other IDs**: a Crashlytics installation UUID, a Firebase installation ID and the device model.
  These identifiers are random and created for the app installation; they are not your name, email, phone number
  or advertising ID.

Crash reports never include what you typed into the app (project, room or wall names, client names, notes, paint
products, colors or color codes, or sizes you typed).

We use crash reports only to keep the app working (app functionality) and to analyze crashes. Google processes this
data on our behalf as a service provider; we do not share it with anyone else and do not sell it. The data is
encrypted in transit (HTTPS). Crashlytics keeps crash reports and the identifiers attached to them for 90 days, then
removes them from its systems.

### Turning crash reports off

Crash reports are optional. Open **Settings** in the app and switch off **Send crash reports**. When you switch it
off, the app stops recording its own error reports and deletes the reports that were not sent yet. The change takes
full effect the next time the app starts. You can use every feature of the app with crash reports off.

## Feedback email

If you send us feedback from the app, it opens your email app. We receive what you write and your email address.
We use them only to answer you, and we delete them on request.

## Deleting your data

- **Crash reports**: switch off **Send crash reports** to stop new reports and delete the ones not sent yet.
  Reports already sent carry no name or email, so we cannot tell which are yours; Crashlytics deletes them after
  90 days. To ask us to delete your feedback emails, write to the contact address above.
- **Data on your device**: uninstalling the app, or clearing its data in Android settings, removes your projects
  and settings from the device.

## Android backup

Android may back up the app's data (your projects, settings and the early-user status) to your Google account,
according to your own Android backup settings. This backup is handled by Google under your account settings; we
cannot see it.

## Children

TapeList is not directed at children.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date.
